# Licensing Lite Classify under other terms

Lite Classify is free and open-source software under the GNU General Public License v3 with the
additional attribution terms in `NOTICE.md`. For most users — researchers, students,
government offices, NGOs, consultants producing maps — that licence is all you need: you may
use Lite Classify for any purpose, including paid work, and the maps you make are yours.

The GPL does require that if you **distribute** Lite Classify or a program built on it, you also
release that program's complete source code under the GPL.

## When you need a separate licence

Contact the author for a separate written licence if you want to:

- build Lite Classify, or parts of its code, into a product whose source you will not release;
- distribute a modified version without the GPL's source-code obligations;
- use the Lite Classify name or icon for your product;
- arrange custom development, integration, training or support.

Licences are agreed case by case, with terms and fees to suit the use.
